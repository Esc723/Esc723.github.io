What the add-on does

The add-on is a Google Sheets editor add-on. It reads issues, merge requests, and (on the planned paid plan) time logs from a GitLab host you choose, and writes that data into a Google spreadsheet that you control. It does not write your sheet edits back to GitLab.

A more detailed description is in the help page. How information is stored is described in the privacy policy.
